⚠ Problem 1 — The Speed Penalty

Google Actively Penalises Slow Websites in Rankings

Page speed is a direct Google ranking factor since 2021. Sites slower than 0.8s TTFB are classified "Needs Improvement" — meaning they rank lower than faster competitors for the same search terms. 1 in 4 local scaffolding sites in Bangalore is losing SEO position every day because of this.

💡 What this costs them: scaffoldingonrentals.com loads in 1.83s — it is being outranked by every faster competitor for the same keywords, purely because of server speed. AAA's Next.js site will load in under 0.4s — among the fastest scaffolding websites in Bangalore by a wide margin.
