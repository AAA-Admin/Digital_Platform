## Why We Do Not Use Templates

This is one of the most important decisions we make for you, and we want you to fully understand it.

**What a template is:** Platforms like Wix, Squarespace, or WordPress themes are pre-built designs used by tens of thousands of businesses. You pick a layout, change the text and colours, and publish.

**Why we refuse to use them for AAA Events:**

1. **Speed.** Templates load 50–200 unnecessary scripts, stylesheets, and plugins that your website doesn't need. A typical Wix or WordPress site loads in 3–6 seconds. We are committing to loading your site in **under 0.8 seconds** (see our guarantee below). This is simply not achievable with a template.

2. **SEO ceiling.** Google's Core Web Vitals scoring directly penalizes slow, bloated pages. A template-built site will always fight against a structural SEO disadvantage. Our custom code is built to score 95–100 on Google PageSpeed from day one.

3. **Brand authority.** Your clients are large-scale event organizers spending lakhs on production. The moment they land on a Wix-looking page, the trust drops. A custom-designed page communicates the same digital seriousness as the companies you want to compete with.

4. **Ownership.** When you use Wix or Squarespace, you are renting space on their platform. We build your site on infrastructure that you own—your domain, your hosting account, your data.

## SEO — What "Basic SEO & Indexing" Covers

For ₹28,000 this is what is included in your SEO:

- **Google Search Console setup** — site verified and submitted to Google
- **XML Sitemap** — auto-generated and submitted so Google finds all your pages
- **On-Page SEO** — title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, and image alt tags optimised for your target keywords
- **Target Keywords** — we will research and optimise for terms like:
  - *"event production company Bengaluru"*
  - *"LED wall rental Bengaluru"*
  - *"scaffolding setup Bengaluru"*
  - *"concert stage setup company"*
- **Schema Markup** — structured data that tells Google you are a local business (helps with the "Map Pack" results)
- **Core Web Vitals compliance** — passing Google's speed and usability tests, which directly affects rankings
- **robots.txt** — correct crawl instructions so Google indexes only what matters

**What is NOT included in this phase** (available in future phases):
- Google My Business optimisation (local map ranking)
- Ongoing monthly content / blog strategy
- Backlink building campaigns
- AI search optimisation (ChatGPT / Perplexity citations)
