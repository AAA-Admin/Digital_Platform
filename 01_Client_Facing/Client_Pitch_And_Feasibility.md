# AAA Events & Production: Project Proposal

This proposal outlines the timeline and investment for the AAA Events digital platform. We have curated a suite of premium features designed specifically to capture high-value leads and present the brand with the same digital authority as an enterprise organization.

---

## 1. Premium Immersive Web Design
A highly visual, lightning-fast landing page designed to establish massive brand trust instantly. We will employ advanced front-end techniques typically reserved for top-tier tech companies.

*   **Business Impact:** Instantly validates your company to high-end clients. A premium feel directly correlates with higher conversion rates for event production.
*   **Key Features:** 
    *   **Scroll-Triggered Animations** (Dynamic text and images revealing as the user scrolls).
    *   **Custom SVG Morphing** (The AAA logo will feature a dynamic animation where the right-side semi-ring smoothly animates or morphs as the page loads).
    *   **Founders Section & Location Integration** (Building trust and showing your Bangalore coverage with Google Maps).
*   **Time to Build:** 15 – 20 Hours
*   **Investment:** ₹25,000 - ₹35,000

---

## 2. Omnichannel Lead Capture (WhatsApp & Web Forms)
Event clients prefer texting over calling. We will implement a dual-strategy capture system.

*   **Business Impact:** Increases lead capture by capturing intent immediately through the channel the client prefers most (WhatsApp).
*   **Key Features:**
    *   "Click-to-Chat" feature that launches an automated WhatsApp Bot flow to instantly collect their event requirements.
    *   A seamless, smart web-form fallback for users who are on desktop or prefer not to use WhatsApp.
*   **Time to Build:** 10 – 15 Hours
*   **Investment:** ₹15,000 - ₹20,000

---

## 3. Intelligent Lead Management & AI Routing
Never miss a lead and always be prepared before you even speak to the client.

*   **Business Impact:** All three owners have 100% visibility on incoming business. You will appear incredibly prepared and professional during the first follow-up call.
*   **Key Features:**
    *   All leads are securely routed to a centralized database (accessible via Google Sheets or a secure Dashboard).
    *   Instant Email Routing: When a lead comes in (e.g., "Need a setup for A.R. Rahman"), our system uses Artificial Intelligence (LLMs) to instantly research the performer/event and summarize that context in the email sent to you.
*   **Time to Build:** 8 – 12 Hours
*   **Investment:** ₹12,000 - ₹18,000

---

## 4. Automated Multi-Owner Scheduling
Eliminates the back-and-forth of finding a time that works for everyone.

*   **Business Impact:** Reduces the time-to-meeting from days to minutes. Secures the client while they are still highly motivated.
*   **Key Features:**
    *   The system checks all 3 of your Google Calendars simultaneously in the background.
    *   It presents the client with a unified calendar showing only the times when at least one (or all) of you are free for a Meet or WhatsApp call.
*   **Time to Build:** 5 – 10 Hours
*   **Investment:** ₹10,000 - ₹15,000

---

## 5. Client Admin Portal (Headless CMS)
Empowers you to manage your own portfolio without paying retainer fees to developers every time you complete a new event.

*   **Business Impact:** Saves you thousands of rupees in long-term developer maintenance fees. Keeps your portfolio fresh and relevant.
*   **Key Features:**
    *   A secure, private portal where you can upload new high-resolution scaffolding images from your phone.
    *   Simple toggle controls to rearrange your galleries and update basic text on the live website instantly.
    *   Built on a "Headless Architecture" meaning the admin panel won't slow down the public-facing website.
*   **Time to Build:** 15 – 20 Hours
*   **Investment:** ₹30,000 - ₹40,000

---

## Infrastructure Strategy (Zero-Maintenance Focus)

To ensure this platform runs flawlessly without requiring expensive monthly server packages, we are utilizing a modern **Serverless Architecture**. 

*   **Domain & Hosting:** We will host the platform on a global edge network (like Vercel), providing enterprise-level speed.
*   **Monthly Server Cost:** Because of the efficient serverless architecture we are building, your fixed monthly hosting and database costs will be virtually **₹0**. 
*   **The "Core Tensor" Difference:** We are actively choosing *not* to use heavy, expensive infrastructure like Kubernetes Auto-scaling for this project, as it would be a massive waste of your monthly budget for standard business traffic. (Note: We *do* deploy Kubernetes for our large-scale clients—such as School Admission Portals where 5,000 parents log in simultaneously to check results and the server cannot crash—but for AAA Events, we have optimized the stack specifically to give you enterprise speed without enterprise recurring costs).
