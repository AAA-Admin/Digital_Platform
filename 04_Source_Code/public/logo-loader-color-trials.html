Direction

This replaces the earlier loader trial. The motion is now built around your clarified ask: one logo becomes three on a diagonal axis, not in a straight horizontal or vertical arrangement, and the text appears after the logo motion finishes.

  • The original loader mock has been removed from this trial page.
  • The loader honors system dark/light theme unless the buttons override it.
  • Dark theme still uses the Sandbox-like blue family best.
  • For light theme, the deeper blues below still hold contrast better than the original bright blue.
